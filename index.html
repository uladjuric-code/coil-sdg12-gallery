OUR CHALLENGE

Electronic waste is growing.

Electronic waste is a growing problem around the world. Many people replace their phones, computers and other electronic devices even when they can still be repaired or reused.

This creates a lot of waste and uses valuable natural resources. We believe that small everyday actions can help protect our planet.

Ula and Ivana want to encourage people to repair, reuse and recycle electronic devices instead of throwing them away.
